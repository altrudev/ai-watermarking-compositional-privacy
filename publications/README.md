## Publication boundary

**Published research object ≠ active research program ≠ experiment ≠ experimental result ≠ validated invariant.**

A publication may describe experimental findings, but publication itself does not promote those findings into production policy, identity-resolution authority, authorship/ownership proof, or a universal privacy law.
